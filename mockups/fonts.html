E · Three font pairings (plus today’s Geist, tuned, for comparison)

Each card shows the same three things: the top bar, the home headline, and a lesson panel with real chemistry symbols. All four are free to use and would be hosted on your own site (no Google Fonts request). The last line of each card is a symbol test: chemistry needs subscripts, Greek letters, and arrows that many fonts don’t include. (In these previews, subscript digits and arrows come from a fallback font for all four, because Google’s delivery strips them. Hosting the font ourselves lets us keep them.)
